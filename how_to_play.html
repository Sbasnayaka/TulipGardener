How To Play

🪴 1. Select a Difficulty Mode

From the Menu, choose how challenging you want your gardening session to be:

  • Beginner - Unlimited time. No countdown. Perfect for learning!
  • Intermediate - You have only 5 seconds to answer.
  • Pro - You have only 3 seconds to answer. For experts!

🧩 2. Solve the Emoji Puzzle

A puzzle image is shown using emoji math equations. Each emoji represents a number. Your goal is to figure out the value of the Heart ❤️ symbol from the given equations. Type your answer in the input box and click Submit!

🌷 3. Grow Your Tulip Garden

Every correct answer plants a new colourful Tulip in your personal Garden on the Dashboard. Watch your garden bloom with each solved puzzle!

⏱️ 4. Chase Your Best Record

Your Best Record is the shortest time (in seconds) you took to correctly solve any puzzle. Solve faster to beat your own record visible on your Profile page!

🏆 5. Climb the Leaderboard

Your score (total correct answers) is ranked globally. Visit the Leaderboard page to see how you compare against other gardeners around the world!
