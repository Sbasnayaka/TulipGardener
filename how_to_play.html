How To Play

🪴 1. Select a Mode

Start in the Menu to choose your difficulty: Beginner (Unlimited Time), Intermediate (30s), or Pro (10s).

🧩 2. Solve the Puzzle

Look at the image given which represents a math puzzle using emojis. Find the value of the Heart (❤️) symbol.

🌷 3. Grow Your Garden

If you answer correctly, a new Tulip is added to your Garden. The faster you solve it on higher difficulties, the better your best time!
